The breed, plainly

Leg length, explained

Munchkin legs come in three lengths. Knowing the difference helps you choose with your eyes open. Photos are representative; each kitten listing has its own photos.

Standard short legs

The classic Munchkin: legs noticeably shorter than a typical cat, still agile and quick. Most of our kittens are this type.

Super short ("rug hugger")

The shortest legs, with the most waddle. Adorable, and worth extra care with ramps, weight and vet checks.

Non-standard (normal legs)

Born to Munchkin parents but without the short-leg gene. Same sweet nature, and a good choice if you prefer a typical cat build.
