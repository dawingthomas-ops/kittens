A small cattery, run with big care

American Munchkin House raises Munchkin kittens at home, in small litters, and tells families the whole truth about the breed.

How we raise our kittens

Our kittens are born in our living space and handled every day from their first week. They meet visitors, hear household noise and learn gentle manners long before they leave. A kitten who feels safe around people grows into a calm, confident companion.

Honest about the breed

Munchkins are delightful, and the short-leg gene is also debated. We breed Munchkin cats only to normal-leg cats, keep our kittens lean and give every family a written health guarantee. We would rather you ask hard questions now than be surprised later. Our breed guide explains the details.

Fair, transparent pricing

What we promise

  • Kittens go home at 12 weeks or older, never earlier.
  • You can see your kitten, the parents and the living space on video before you commit.
  • Every kitten leaves with health records, a contract and lifetime breeder support.
  • If life changes and you can no longer keep your cat, call us first.
